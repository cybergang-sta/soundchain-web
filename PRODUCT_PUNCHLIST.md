# SoundChain Product Feature Punchlist

Working inventory of differentiated features for the SoundChain pitch. Grouped by thesis, with rough build complexity (S/M/L) and current mockup status.

Legend: **Mocked** = represented on the live prototype · **Planned** = spec'd, not built · **Investigating** = needs product/legal research before committing.

---

## Fan Engagement and Ownership

| Feature | Idea | Complexity | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Royalty Share "Portfolio" view | Treat fan NFT holdings like an investment portfolio: total accrued royalties over time, per-artist performance chart, "up 12% in plays this month" chips | S | **Mocked** (home: Royalty Portfolio tiles + play-change chips) |
| Prediction markets on rising artists | Stake SOUND on which "Now Rising" entries hit a 30-day listen threshold; winners split a small pool; feeds real A&R signal | M | Planned |
| Co-ownership drops | Two artists mint a joint NFT with the royalty split baked into the contract from day one | M | Planned |
| "First Fan" badges | Permanent non-transferable on-chain credential for the first N buyers of an artist's first NFT | S | **Mocked** (home: First Fan badges) |
| Streak-based SOUND multipliers | Listening streaks compound into fee discounts / early-drop access windows, not just token drips | S | Planned |
